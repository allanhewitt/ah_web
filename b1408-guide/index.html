BSc Sport & Physical Activity — Year Four

B1408 Practical Project

A year-long, self-directed placement project: identify a real need with an organisation, design and deliver an intervention, then evaluate what happened.

At a glance

You choose a placement provider — usually somewhere you already have a connection — identify a genuine need of theirs, then design, implement and evaluate an intervention to meet it. The work culminates in a written report and a digital presentation.

  • Module leaderDr Allan Hewitt — allan.hewitt@strath.ac.uk
  • Report3,000 words, worth 60% of the module grade
  • Presentation15 minutes, digital, worth 25% of the module grade
  • Placement evaluationCompleted by your supervisor, worth 15% of the module grade
Compulsory, ungraded steps: your project proposal must be approved before you start, and a mid-placement review must be completed. Missing either results in 0% for the whole module, regardless of your other marks.

The process, step by step

1

Choose a placement provider

An organisation with a recognisable management structure, active in physical activity or sports development, able to assign you a project supervisor.

2

Identify a genuine need

Find a specific challenge or objective facing the provider or its client group, and show evidence it's real and aligned with the provider's own aims.

3

Design the intervention

Plan an original activity or programme that responds to the need — not simply delivering something the provider already runs.

4

Get the proposal approved

Submit online by 27 November 2026. You must have written confirmation before starting any delivery.

5

Deliver and hold a mid-placement review

Implement the project, staying in regular contact with your module leader, and complete a formal review meeting with your supervisor.

6

Evaluate, present and report

Assess the outcome using proper evidence, then submit the presentation and written report by their respective deadlines.
